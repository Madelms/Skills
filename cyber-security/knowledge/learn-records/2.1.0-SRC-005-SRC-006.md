## Dedupe record for the new rules

- **AUTHN-008** (factor skipped / temporary token accepted). Closest: **AUTHN-001** (a protected operation has no or bypassable authentication; does not address multi-step flow state), **AUTHN-005** (a code can be guessed, replayed or abused; does not cover skipping the factor or promotion), **AUTHN-002** (token validation incomplete; does not cover tokens issued too early). Different mechanism: flow integrity.
- **DATA-004** (token in URL or to an untrusted origin). Closest: **FE-002** (where a token is stored in the browser), **DATA-001** (secrets written to logs, audit or e-mail by the system), **INPUT-006** (URL scope allow-list for navigation). Different mechanism: transport of a credential through a URL or to a third-party origin.

## Rejected as project-specific or not generic
- Endpoint names, route families, entity and business terminology, identifiers, hostnames, numeric record values.
- Individual severity ratings and statuses from the source reports.
- Platform-specific constraints of one hosting service (recorded only as the generic "a platform block is not a fix").
- One-per-finding rules: none created. Of 46 findings, 34 mechanism rows result and only two required new rules.
